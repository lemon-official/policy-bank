# ORG-0001 Artifact gates

## Context
Stages hand off through committed artifacts (intent, spec, plan).

## Decision
Approval is a merge reviewed by path owners. Every repo's CODEOWNERS must own /docs/changes/ and /docs/adr/.

## Consequences
Enforced by the control(s) in org-controls.yaml that cite ORG-0001.
